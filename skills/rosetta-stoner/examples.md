## Sanskrit Dharani — Eleven-Faced Avalokiteśvara Heart Dharani Sutra

# Eleven-Faced Avalokiteśvara Heart Dharani Sutra

## Invocation

_Namo Ratna Trayāya_
Homage to the Triple Gem (Buddha, Dharma, and Sangha).

_Namo Ārya Jñāna Sāgara Vairochana Vyūharāja Tathāgatāya_
Homage to the noble, all-knowing Ocean of Wisdom, Vairochana Tathāgata (the cosmic Buddha of luminosity and truth).

_Arhate, Samyaksam Buddhāya_
To the Worthy One, the Perfectly Enlightened One.

_Namo Sarva Tathāgate Bhyah, Arhata Bhyah, Samyaksam Buddhe Bhyah_
Homage to all the Tathāgatas (those who have "thus gone"), Arhats (worthy ones), and Perfect Buddhas.

_Namo Ārya Avalokiteśvarāya Bodhisattvāya, Mahā Sattvāya, Mahā Karuṇikāya_
Homage to the noble Avalokiteśvara Bodhisattva (the awakening being who hears the cries of the world), the Great Being, the Great Compassionate One.

_Tadyathā: Om Dara Dara, Diri Diri, Duru Duru_
Thus: Om — bring forth, bring forth! Expand, expand! Flow, flow!

_Itte We, Itte Chale Chale, Purachale Purachale_
Go, go; proceed, proceed; fulfill, fulfill!

_Kusume Kusuma Vare, Ili Mili, Chiti Jvālam, Apanaye Svāhā_
In the flower, in the flower's radiance, arising, blazing with wisdom — may it be so!
